## Takeaways
- Top practice bots are aggressive: first deaths at 9 s, 9 of 16 dead by ~114 s. **Not dying early is worth ~7–9 points per match by itself.**
- 0-kill survivors placed 3rd and 5th. Placement comes from survival; kills matter mainly as cube income for the endgame.
- The winner snowballed: kills give the victim's cubes + 2. 31 cubes = 1.4× HP and damage (stats cap at 10 cubes), plus upgrades and 2 supply drops. Expect one snowballed killer at the end; beating it, or outlasting it, decides 1st vs 2nd.
- Most top bots run gun+laser. Some run gun+lightning.
